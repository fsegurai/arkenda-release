UX guidelines

A plugin should feel like part of Arkenda. For sandboxed plugins most of this is automatic, because the app renders their views, settings and menus with its own components; what is left is the words, the structure and the choices you make.

Quick rules

  • Short, plain titles. Name commands as "Plugin: action" ("Git: pull"); the palette groups them.
  • One tool, one job. A panel or widget answers one question; split it rather than adding tabs.
  • Quiet by default. No notification for routine work; reserve them for what the user asked for.
  • Settings only when needed. A plugin with no settings shows just its switch; that is good.
  • Never block. Show a "Counting…" state at once and update the view when work is done.

Design tokens and themes

The app's look comes from design tokens (--ark-*: colours, fonts, radii, line heights). A sandboxed plugin's views use them automatically, in light and dark. A theme sets token values only (THEME_TOKENS), with text at 4.5:1 contrast at least on the background; see Surfaces ▸ Themes.

Components

  • Sandboxed plugins describe views as data (SandboxView): headings, text with a muted or strong tone, lists, note and tag links, buttons. The app draws them with its own components, so they match the app and follow its accessibility rules.
  • Built-in plugins use the shared components of @arkenda/kit/ui (buttons, toggles, menus, dialogs, icons, trees) and the app's tokens, never one-off styles.

Words and languages

Write titles, labels and descriptions in plain English, in sentence case, without jargon.

Not available yet: translations of a third-party plugin's own text. Manifest titles and view text are shown as written; the app's own words around them follow the user's language.

Accessibility

  • Give every button a label that says what it does ("Refresh", not "Go").
  • Don't rely on colour alone: say it in the text too.
  • Keep text short enough for the view limits (The sandbox); long text wraps.
  • Hotkeys are suggestions: users can rebind them, and the app's keys always win.

Mobile

On phones, menus become bottom sheets, a status bar tool opens as a sheet, and touch targets are at least 44 px (--ark-touch-target). List mobile in platforms only after trying your plugin at a phone width; keep views short enough to read without scrolling sideways.
