Reminders

Arkenda can remind you of a task on its due day at the time you give it, and of any note at a day and time you pick. A reminder is part of the note's text, so it syncs with the note to your other devices, and each device shows it once.

A task's reminder

A task reminds you when its due date has a time. Write the time after the due date, as ⏰ and the time on a 24-hour clock:

- [ ] Call the bank 📅 2026-11-20 ⏰ 09:30
  • A due date without a time, like every task written before, reminds you of nothing.
  • A time without a due date does nothing.
  • In Todos, a task with a due date has a ⏰ button beside its date: pick a time, change it, or Clear it.

A note's reminder

Open a note's … menu and choose Remind me…. Pick a day and a time, then Save. Arkenda keeps it in the note's remind property, in the note's front matter:

remind: 2026-11-20T09:30

The time is your device's time. To remove the reminder, open Remind me… again and choose Clear: the remind key leaves the front matter, and nothing else in the note changes.

When a reminder fires

You get a notification with the note's title and the task. Where the notification has buttons of its own (the Linux and Windows desktops, Android), they are the reminder's buttons; elsewhere a card at the top of the window shows the reminder with them:

  • Open opens the note, at the task's line for a task.
  • Snooze 10 min reminds you again in 10 minutes, on this device only.
  • Done ticks the task, or removes a note's reminder. In a vault someone shared with you to read only, Done opens the note instead.

A reminder fires once on each device. If the app was closed when a reminder was due, it fires when you open the app, as long as it was due less than a day before.

On the desktop

The app's own clock fires reminders while Arkenda is open, also after the computer wakes from sleep or after the clock changes. On Linux the notification comes from the desktop's own notifications, and on Windows it is a toast. It has Snooze 10 min and Done buttons, which work like the card's; a click on the notification itself opens the note and brings Arkenda forward. In a vault shared with you to read only, it has no Done. Where the desktop's notifications show no buttons, the card waits in the app instead.

On the web

The app's own clock fires reminders while the page is open. The first time you set a reminder, the browser asks whether Arkenda may show notifications, and it asks only that once:

  • Allow: each reminder also shows a browser notification; a click on it opens the note.
  • Block, or a browser that does not ask: reminders show only the card in the app, and Arkenda never asks again. To change your mind later, allow notifications for the site in your browser's settings.

On Android

Android keeps your next reminders, so they fire with Arkenda closed and after the phone restarts. The notification has Open, Snooze and Done. Snooze works without opening the app. Open and Done open Arkenda. The first reminder asks for the notification permission.

Android 14 and later deliver a reminder at its minute only when you allow Alarms & reminders for Arkenda in Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Arkenda. Without it, Android may deliver a reminder a few minutes late while the phone is idle. When you set a reminder and it is not allowed, Arkenda explains this with a button that opens that page. Once you allow it, or choose Dismiss, the notice does not come back.
