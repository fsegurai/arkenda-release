# Arkenda 0.5.2

Arkenda is a local-first, private Markdown notes app. There is no account and no telemetry: notes stay on your
device as plain Markdown, and nothing reaches the network unless you turn on something that needs it, such as
sync between your own devices.

This is the documentation of version 0.5.2, released on 2026-11-21. Pick another version to read what it shipped.
