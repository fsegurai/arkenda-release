Your notes, on your devices. Private, fast and yours.

Arkenda is a local-first Markdown notes app with encrypted device sync, a whiteboard, media, todos and a calendar, on Windows, Linux, Android and the web.

Download ▶ Try it in your browser

Free · No account · Your files stay plain Markdown · Other platforms

Everything in one quiet place

Write, plan and think, without handing your notes to a server.

  • 🔒

    Local-first, private

    Notes live on your device as plain Markdown files or in your browser. No account, no tracking.

  • 🔄

    Encrypted sync

    Sync your devices directly over the local network or the internet, end-to-end encrypted. Optional S3 backup you control.

  • 🧩

    Whiteboard

    Shapes, stickies, connectors, mind maps and note cards on an infinite board.

  • 🗓️

    Todos, calendar, Today

    Tasks and events right inside your notes, gathered into one view of your day.

  • 🎞️

    Media library

    Images, audio, video and PDFs with previews, plus voice memos.

  • 🔌

    Plugins

    Extend Arkenda with sandboxed plugins: panels, commands, widgets and more.
